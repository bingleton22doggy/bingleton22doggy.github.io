The game

Every corridor leads somewhere unfamiliar.

Shattered Memories is a first-person survival horror game built around shifting, procedurally generated hallways. Explore the dark, search rooms for supplies, and avoid the presence moving just beyond your view. Your progress stays in your browser; no account or game server is required.
